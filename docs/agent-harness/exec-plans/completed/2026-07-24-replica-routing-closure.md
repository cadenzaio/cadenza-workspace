# Replica Routing And Sender-Side Load-Balancing Closure

Date: 2026-07-24

## Goal

- Outcome: close the existing sender-side replica-routing feature with bounded
  selection state and focused behavioral proof across delegation, signals, and
  a real three-Cell environment.
- Why it matters: replicas must absorb distribution complexity without asking
  authored business logic to know placement topology or introducing a separate
  load-balancing service.

## Current Status

- State: `done`
- Current repo: `cadenza-workspace`
- Impacted repos: `cadenza-chamber`, `cadenza-cell`, `cadenza-workspace`
- Approved design:
  [sender-side replica routing closure](../../../decisions/2026-07-24-sender-side-replica-routing-closure.md)

## Scope

- In scope:
  - bounded per-route-group, per-current-epoch round-robin state;
  - exact focused delegation and signal selection sequences;
  - independent route-group state and route-epoch reset;
  - exact Cell resolution of either selected replica without substitution;
  - existing three-Cell Linux proof extended with four balanced delegations;
  - durable evidence proving both remote replicas participate;
  - concise contract and closure documentation.
- Out of scope:
  - weighted, least-loaded, latency-aware, or globally coordinated balancing;
  - a separate load-balancer, gateway, broker, or service-discovery component;
  - automatic retry after started or uncertain execution;
  - actor-owner balancing;
  - a new system-test harness, performance benchmark, schema, or migration.

## Questions Or Blockers

- Focused routing implementation and proof are complete.
- The extended Linux scenario durably proved balanced execution and progressed
  through a provider restart and a fifth successful delegation. Its unchanged
  final lifecycle phase could not complete because the restarted supply process
  exited after a managed Cell violated the existing empty-custody lifecycle
  transition invariant.
- The user approved independent routing closure on 2026-07-24. The lifecycle
  problem was resolved in the completed
  [supply-restart hardening plan](./2026-07-24-supply-restart-lifecycle-hardening.md).

## Assumptions

- The existing three-Cell pre-enrolled supply scenario remains the smallest
  honest system proof because it already converges two target replicas on
  separate Cells.
- Exact sequence is proved at the synchronous adapter boundary; the system
  layer proves participation from durable evidence without interpreting
  asynchronous custody order as routing order.

## Exit Criteria

- Four focused delegations select two candidates in exact alternating order.
- Repeated signal forwarding selects one replica per subscribing slice and
  alternates independently inside each group.
- Route epoch change resets bounded selection state.
- Cell tests prove both explicit destinations and stale-selection rejection.
- Four real system delegations remain topology-free and durable evidence names
  both target replicas or their exact route-member identities.
- No failed or uncertain started execution is retried elsewhere.
- Complete affected-repo validation passes, with any unavailable privileged
  Linux proof reported explicitly rather than simulated.
